Makeup Course Options

The Basic Makeup Training Course consists of theory & 8 hours of practical learning at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. The course covers theory and makeup on self including contouring, lips, eyes and brows. Students looking to complete this course do not need a model ready on the day of the workshop.

The Master Makeup Training Course consists of an a total of 18 hours of workshop which covers makeup application on others. It is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. Students must provide 3 models for this workshop

Spray Tanning Course Options

The spray tanning course consists of a 6 hour workshop which covers contouring, theory, face and body spray tanning. It is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location.
