Eyebrow Training Courses

The Microblading Training course consists of theory & 20 hours of practical learning at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. There is no online option for practical training. Unfortunately, there is no practical location for this course in Guelph or Kitchener.

The Eyebrow Lamination, Lift and Tint course consists of an additional 6 hour workshop which is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. This course is also available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.

The Eyelash Waxing course consists of theory & 5 hour workshop available at our St. Catharines/ Niagara location. This course is also available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.

The Eyebrow Threading course consists of a 5 hour workshop which is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. This course is not available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.

We highly recommend attending training in person as most beauty skills require hands on practice. Work with our professional teachers and program director today at the National Association of Estheticians.
