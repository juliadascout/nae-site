The Master Brow Artist: Microblading, Brow Lamination, Lift and Tint Artist Course Training | Beauty Certification Programs

The Master Brow Artist: Microblading, Waxing, Lift and Tint course is a combination of several popular brow artist services. The focus of this course is to provide students with enough skills to perform nearly all brow services. This course  will go into detail on health and safety, business tips, tool kit, skin anatomy, blades, pigments, microblading, brow waxing, brow tint, brow lift, practice questions, client forms and key terms. This  50-hour course will also involve shadowing the procedures, practical experience, a 50-question exam and a practical exam to receive the Master Brow Artist Certificate.
