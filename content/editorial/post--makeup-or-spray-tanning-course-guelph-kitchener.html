Makeup Course Options

The Basic Makeup Training Course consists of theory & 8 hours of practical learning at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. The course covers theory and makeup on self including contouring, lips, eyes and brows. There is not an online option for practical training. Students looking to complete this course do not need a model ready on the day of the workshop. Unfortunately, there is no practical location for this course in Guelph or Kitchener.

The Master Makeup Training Course consists of an a total of 18 hours of workshop which covers makeup application on others. It is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. Students must provide 3 models for this workshop. Unfortunately, this course is not available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.

Spray Tanning Course Options

The spray tanning course consists of a 6 hour workshop which covers contouring, theory, face and body spray tanning. It is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. This course is not available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.
