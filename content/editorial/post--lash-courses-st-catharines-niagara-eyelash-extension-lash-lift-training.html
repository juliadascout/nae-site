Lash Courses at NAE

The Eyelash Extension Classic Lash Training course consists of theory & 16 hours of practical learning at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. A student may be eligible to do a final practical test online if they are unavailable to attend the in person training on available dates.

The Volume Eyelash Extension Training course consists of an additional 5 hour workshop which is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. Students may not be eligible for online practical test.

The Eyelash Lift and Tint Training course consists of theory & 5 hour workshop available at our St. Catharines/ Niagara location. This course is also available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.

We highly recommend attending training in person as most beauty skills require hands on practice. Work with our professional teachers and program director today at the National Association of Estheticians.

Proper lash training is important to ensure client safety. Lashes are extremely close to the mucous membrane and so there is a risk of infection. Clients also may be allergic to the lash glue which will cause allergic reaction. Know the risks first and work with a trained professional!
