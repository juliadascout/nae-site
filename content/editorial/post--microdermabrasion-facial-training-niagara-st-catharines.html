Microdermabrasion Course Options

The Microdermabrasion Training course consists of theory & 5 hours of practical learning at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. There is an online option for practical training via zoom in any zone. Students looking to complete this course online must have a model ready on the day of the online workshop. Students are required to bring 2 models to the practical learning.

Facial Course Options

The Facial course consists of an additional 6 hour workshop which is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. This course teaches skin types, steps, extraction and facial massage, This course is not available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.
