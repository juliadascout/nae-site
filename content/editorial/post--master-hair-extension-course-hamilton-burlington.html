Hair Extension Course Options

There are a variety of hair extension courses offered at our beauty school. The courses consists of theory & practical learning at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. The instructor is available for mobile training in the Hamilton, Burlington area.

Fusion Hair Extension Training Course

This course covers theory training and fusion hair extension training. The student is required to attend 3 in person workshops, 3-5 hours per session. There is not an online option for practical training. Students looking to complete this course need a model ready on 1 day of the workshop. The instructor is available for mobile training in Hamilton & Burlington.
