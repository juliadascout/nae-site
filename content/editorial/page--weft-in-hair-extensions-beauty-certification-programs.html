The Hair Industry

The hair industry is a multibillion-dollar market. Hair extensions have been becoming more popular ever since the 80’s. If hair extensions are not installed properly, they can cause damage to the hair as well as hair loss. A proper hair extension training course is necessary to educate and train a professional hair extension technician. Fusion hair extensions involve melting pre-bonded keratin hair extensions to natural hair using a heated wand. Fusion hair extensions allows washing and styling hair. Fusion hair extensions need re-positioning every 2–3 months as the natural hair grows, however the bonds can last up to 6 months. Fusion hair extensions are the longest lasting hair extension method. Fusion hair extensions are for clients who want a long-lasting extension and have extra time in them to manage the aftercare. 

Weft hair extensions are a versatile and popular choice for individuals looking to add length, volume, and style to their natural hair. These extensions consist of hair strands that are sewn or bonded together along a thin strip, also known as the weft. Weft hair extensions come in various lengths, textures, colors, and styles, making them suitable for a wide range of hair types and preferences.

One of the key advantages of weft hair extensions is their seamless blend with natural hair, creating a natural and undetectable look. They can be styled, washed, and treated just like natural hair, offering flexibility and ease of maintenance. Additionally, weft extensions are known for their durability and longevity, making them a cost-effective investment for those seeking long-term hair enhancement.

Weft hair extensions can be applied using different methods, including sewing, bonding, or using clips, providing options for individuals with various preferences and hair types. Whether you desire added length, increased volume, or a transformative hairstyle, weft hair extensions offer a versatile and customizable solution to achieve your desired look. Overall, weft hair extensions are a popular choice among hairstylists and clients alike for their natural appearance, versatility, and longevity.
