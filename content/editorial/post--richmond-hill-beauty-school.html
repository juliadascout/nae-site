The National Association of Estheticians Richmond Hill Beauty School location is a beauty school offering professional esthetics and cosmetology training for aspiring beauty professionals across Ontario. With flexible online and in-class learning options, NAE provides hands-on certification programs designed to help students build successful careers in the beauty industry.

Located on Reid road in Richmond Hill, Ontario, the National Association of Estheticians serves students throughout the Greater Toronto Area and surrounding communities including Vaughan, Markham, Thornhill, Aurora, Newmarket, North York, Scarborough, Mississauga, Brampton, and Toronto.

Students receive practical education, small class instruction, and industry-focused training to prepare for careers in salons, spas, mobile beauty services, and entrepreneurship.
