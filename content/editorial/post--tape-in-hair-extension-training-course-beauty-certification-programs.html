The Hair Industry

The hair industry is a multibillion-dollar market. Hair extensions have been becoming more popular ever since the 80’s. If hair extensions are not installed properly, they can cause damage to the hair as well as hair loss. A proper hair extension training course is necessary to educate and train a professional hair extension technician.

Tape-in hair extensions are a popular choice for adding length and volume to natural hair. These extensions are made of high-quality human hair that is securely attached to a thin strip of tape. The tape is then placed on either side of a small section of natural hair, allowing it to blend seamlessly with the rest of the hair.

One of the main advantages of tape-in extensions is that they are easy to apply and remove. The process of installing tape-in extensions typically takes around 30-60 minutes, depending on the amount of hair being applied. Unlike other types of extensions that require heat or glue for attachment, tape-ins are a more gentle and non-damaging option.

Tape-in extensions are also known for their natural look and feel. Because the tape lies flat against the scalp, the extensions are virtually undetectable and blend seamlessly with the natural hair. This makes them a popular choice for those looking for a more natural and subtle enhancement.

Additionally, tape-in extensions are relatively low maintenance compared to other types of extensions. They can be easily washed, styled, and cared for just like natural hair. With proper care, tape-in extensions can last for several months before needing to be reinstalled.

Overall, tape-in hair extensions are a versatile and convenient option for those looking to add length and volume to their natural hair. With their natural look, easy application, and low maintenance, it's no wonder why they have become a popular choice among hair extension enthusiasts.

What are Tape in Hair Extensions?

Tape-in hair extensions are attached by placing human hair in between two pieces of hair extensions. They last 1-3 months and the hair can be gently combed and washed while wearing the extensions. This method of installment is for client who want extensions that can be easily installed and taken off.
