Sugaring Course Options

The Basic Body Sugaring Training course consists of theory & 6 hours of practical learning at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. The course covers underarm sugaring, limb sugaring, face and brow sugaring and leg sugaring. There is not an online option for practical training. Students looking to complete this course must have a model ready on the day of the workshop.

The Basic Body & Brazilian Training course consists of an additional 3 hour workshop which covers brazilian sugaring on women. It is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. Students must provide a model for this workshop. Unfortunately, this course is not available online.

The Master Sugaring Training course consists of all of the above plus an additional 2 hour training to cover brazilian sugaring on men. It is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. Students must provide a model for this workshop. Unfortunately, this course is not available online.
