National Association of Estheticians Company History

Kalleigh Sharp, an entrepreneur and esthetician, saw a lack of affordable, quality beauty training in the area. The beauty colleges she researched were either in Toronto or expensive, with little content or student support. She founded the National Association of Estheticians Inc. in 2020 to make professional beauty education more affordable, flexible and accessible. Today NAE trains students at its St. Catharines, Niagara Falls and Richmond Hill studios.
