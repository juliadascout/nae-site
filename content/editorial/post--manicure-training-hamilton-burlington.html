Nail Technician Course Options

The Basic Nail Manicure Technician Training course consists of theory & 8 hours of practical learning at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. The course covers cuticle cleaning, basic polish and basic nail care. There is not an online option for practical training. Students looking to complete this course must have a model ready on the day of the workshop. Unfortunately, there is no practical location for this course in Hamilton or Burlington.

The Master Nail Technician Manicure Training course consists of an additional 8 hour workshop which covers tips, acrylic, gels and shaping. It is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. Unfortunately, this course is not available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.

The Grand Master Nail Technician Manicure Training course consists of all of the above plus additional 8 hour workshop which covers pedicures, spa manicures and design. It is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. This course is not available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.
