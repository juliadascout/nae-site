Microdermabrasion Facial Courses Toronto Mississauga

The Microdermabrasion Training course consists of theory & 5 hours of practical learning at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. There is an online option for practical training via zoom. Students looking to complete this course online must have a model ready on the day of the online workshop. Unfortunately, there is no practical location for this course in Toronto or Mississauga.

The Facial course consists of an additional 6 hour workshop which is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. This course teaches skin types, steps, extraction and facial massage, This course is not available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.
