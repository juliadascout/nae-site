Brow Lamination, Lift and Tint Certifications

The Brow Lamination, Lift and Tint Artist Course Training is available for $599 + hst. The course is being offered online and in St. Catharines.

A full brow lift and tint treatment includes shaping the brows, waxing or sugaring, tweezing, tinting and brow lamination. Originating in the U.K. eyebrow lamination is sweeping the nation. It involves straightening the brow hairs using several solutions to create a full, thick, slicked, defined, fluffy brow shape.

This Brow Lamination, Lift and Tint Artist Training Manual will go into detail on health and safety, business tips, brow lamination, brow tint, brow shaping, the tool kit, hair anatomy, the brow lift  process, practice questions, document guides and key terms. This 20-hour course will also involve shadowing a brow lift artist, practical experience, a 20-question exam and a practical exam to receive the Brow Lift and Tint Artist Certificate.
