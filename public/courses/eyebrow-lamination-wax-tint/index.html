Common questions

What is included in the fee?

Your sessions with the trainer, the materials used during them, and a certificate of completion.

Do I have to buy the kit?

No. The kit is optional and is not part of the course fee. The price is the same without it.

Do I need experience?

No. The course starts from the beginning.

How long does it take?

5-7 hours of instruction, across 1-2 sessions.

What do I get at the end?

A certificate of completion from National Association of Estheticians Inc., issued once you have finished the practical hours and the assessment.

If I pay now, when do I train?

Book your training dates within two weeks of payment.
