National Association of Estheticians Company History

It can be tough to find someone to run a company with but Marysa and Kalleigh are lucky! Both directors of NAE are entrepreneurs and estheticians. Kalleigh’s company, NV Beauty Boutique focuses on promoting cosmetic services. Marysa’s company is a successful beauty salon in Thorold. Her salon provides eyelash and skin care services. Kalleigh provided client referrals to Marysa for a small fee. During a meeting in June 2019, the women discussed the lack of affordable quality training courses in the area. They researched several beauty colleges which were either located in Toronto or expensive with low content or student support. The women partnered together to form the National Association of Estheticians Inc. Both entrepreneurs have their own strengths and are from different backgrounds.
