Description

The Makeup course is complimented with a Starter Kit. Check out this amazing makeup artist starter kit to kick  start your career!

The basic makeup tool kit includes:

–          Make up remover wipes: to remove makeup prior to makeup application –          Brushes: used to apply makeup –          Beauty Blenders: used to apply foundation –          Fake eyelashes –          Lash glue: used for fake eyelashes (can cause allergic reaction) –          Eyeshadow, primer, lipstick, bronzer, highlighter, concealer, blush, lip liner & foundation
