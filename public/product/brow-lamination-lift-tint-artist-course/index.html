Brow Lamination, Lift & tint Artist Course

The Brow Lamination, Lift and Tint Artist Course

 This Brow Lamination, Lift and Tint Artist Training Manual will go into detail on health and safety, business tips, brow lamination, brow tint, brow shaping, the tool kit, hair anatomy, the brow lift  process, practice questions, document guides and key terms. This 20-hour course will also involve shadowing a brow lift artist, practical experience, a 20-question exam and a practical exam to receive the Brow Lift and Tint Artist Certificate.
