The Lash Trend

Long lashes are a beauty trend that draws attention to the eyes. Like they say, beauty is in the “eye” of the beholder. Long lashes create a wakeful look. Eyelash extensions are designed to extend the length, curl and fullness of natural eyelashes. They are made of silk, mink or synthetic mink material (Reid, 2019). They are attached to the natural eyelash with a specially formulated lash extension adhesive.  Lash extensions are available in different lengths, widths and curls, as well as different colours. Classic lash extensions come in both standard (round) or flat (ellipse) styles. For volume lashes there are volume fans (handmade) or premade volume clusters. Other options for fuller lashes include false strip lashes or temporary volume clusters.

Lash extensions have become very popular, making this career a very exciting and fun opportunity. The lash industry is constantly growing and developing. Staying up to date with new products, techniques and services is incredibly important if you wish to have a successful career.

Lash Lifts

Lash Lifts extends the length and curl of natural eyelashes. It is a low-maintenance lash perm that lifts & enhances a client’s natural lashes. These are becoming a common substitute for eyelash extensions.

Eyelash Extension Training Course

The National Association of Estheticians' Eyelash Artist Expert Training course goes into detail on health and safety, business tips, the lash extension tool kit, eyelash anatomy, the lash extension process, lash lift process, practice questions, document guides and key terms. This 20-hour course will also involve shadowing a Lash extension technician, Lash lift technician, practical experience, a 50-question exam and a practical exam to receive the Lash Extension Technician Certificate and starter kit.
