What are Lash Lifts?

Lash lifts have become very popular, making this career a very exciting and fun opportunity. Long lashes are a beauty trend that draws attention to the eyes. Like they say, beauty is in the “eye” of the beholder! Long lashes also create a wakeful look.  Lash lifts are designed to extend the length and curl of natural eyelashes. It is a low-maintenance lash perm that lifts & enhances a client’s natural lashes for up to 6-8 weeks! The lash industry is constantly growing and developing. Staying up to date with current/new products, techniques and services is incredibly important to have a successful career.

 

A lash lift and tint treatment is a popular cosmetic procedure that helps to enhance the appearance of the natural eyelashes. This treatment involves perming and lifting the lashes to give them a more defined and curled look, while also tinting them to make them appear darker and more voluminous.

During a lash lift and tint treatment, the technician will first apply a lifting solution to the lashes, which helps to curl them upwards and give them a more lifted appearance. This solution is left on for a specific amount of time, depending on the desired level of lift, before being removed. The next step is to tint the lashes, which involves applying a dye to the lashes to make them appear darker and more noticeable.

One of the main benefits of a lash lift and tint treatment is that it can help to make the eyes appear more open and awake, without the need for mascara or lash extensions. The results can last for several weeks, making it a low-maintenance option for those looking to enhance their natural lashes.

Overall, a lash lift and tint treatment can be a great way to add definition and volume to the lashes, while also saving time on daily makeup routines. It is a popular choice for those looking to enhance their natural beauty with minimal effort.
