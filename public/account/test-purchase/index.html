Test purchase

Runs everything that happens after a student pays, without Stripe: the enrolment, the student's confirmation email, the course in their account, the administrators' alert and the lead. Every email says [Test] and no money moves. Live checkout is not touched.

You must be signed in on this site with an administrator account.

What to look at

  1. The page the student lands on after paying
  2. The student's inbox: the confirmation email (subject starts with [Test])
  3. Sign out, then sign in as the student on Account: the course is under My courses (the student's email address has to be confirmed first)
  4. Your inbox and Kalleigh's: the New enrolment alert
  5. The apps: Leads, where the student shows as enrolled

When you are done, remove every test purchase (the leads stay; mark them lost on the Leads board if you like).
