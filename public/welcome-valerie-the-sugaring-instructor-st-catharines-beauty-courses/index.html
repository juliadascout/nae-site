Body Sugaring

Body sugaring is a common hair removal process using natural remedies (sugar, lemon & water). The paste is places on your skin and then removed along with the hair and dead skin cells. Sugaring is less painful then washing and you're left with beautiful, smooth skin. Facial Sugaring lasts 2-5 weeks, the rest of the body varies between 3-8 weeks. Sugaring is available at the Thorold Salon or mobile across ontario. 

How do I prepare for a sugaring appointment?

To prepare for your sugaring appointment, keep your skin clean and dry. Avoid applying lotions or other topical products to the area before treatment. Keep your face clear of moisturizer, powder, makeup and lotion. For underarm sugaring: grow out hair for at least one week prior to your appointment. All of our hair removal specialists are certified and educated. Our experts can answer any questions you have and will ensure you're comfortable.
