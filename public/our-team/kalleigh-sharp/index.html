Our team / Kalleigh Sharp

Founder & CEO

Kalleigh Sharp

Kalleigh is the Founder and CEO of the National Association of Estheticians Inc. An entrepreneur, educator and advocate for accessible career education, she writes NAE's curriculum and teaches hair courses at our Richmond Hill beauty school.

Explore programs Book a consultation

About Kalleigh

Kalleigh graduated from the University of Guelph with a degree in Sociology in 2014. Like a lot of graduates, she left with student debt and spent years working several jobs at once to pay it down.

In 2018 she trained in hair extensions, lash extensions and microblading at Pro Beauty Academy, and started building a client list of her own. With no car, she travelled to clients by transit until her bookings filled up and her debt was paid off.

Her journey

In June 2019, Kalleigh sat down with a fellow esthetician and salon owner to talk about a problem they both kept running into. Good beauty training in the region was either in Toronto or expensive, and too often it came with thin course content and little support once the class was over.

They partnered to build NAE around the opposite idea: practical courses taught by people who do the work, at a price a student can manage without taking on debt.

Her vision

Kalleigh built NAE to make professional beauty education more affordable, flexible and accessible: online theory students can work through at home, paired with hands-on, instructor-led workshops at three beauty schools.

Her goal is training that helps people gain practical skills, start their own business and build greater financial independence.

Her mission

Kalleigh knows what it costs to start over with a loan hanging over you. Her aim is for every NAE student to learn a skill they can build on, without the debt she had to work her way out of.
