Tavia Savoury

Tavia is an esthetician and brow artist who runs her own brow studio in Ajax, with experience across skin care, lashes and clinic management.

About Tavia

Tavia has worked as an esthetician since 2020, as a brow specialist for a major cosmetics brand and as the manager of a teaching clinic, where she supported students through their practical hours.

Today she runs her own brow studio, so students learn from someone who does the work for paying clients every week.

What Tavia teaches

  • Manicures, pedicures and gel nails
  • Brow shaping and tinting
  • Threading
  • Makeup
  • Waxing
