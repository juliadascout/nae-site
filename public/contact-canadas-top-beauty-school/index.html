The National Association of Estheticians stands out as Canada’s beauty school because it focuses on what actually matters for success in today’s esthetics industry: practical, career-ready skills delivered in a flexible, modern format. Instead of locking students into long, rigid programs, it offers targeted certifications in high-demand services like lashes, brows, skincare, and nails, allowing students to start earning quickly and build their business step by step. Its hybrid training model blends online theory with hands-on instruction, making it accessible without sacrificing real experience, while small class sizes ensure personalized guidance that many larger institutions can’t match. With a strong emphasis on entrepreneurship, social media marketing, and client-building—not just technique—it prepares students to run successful beauty businesses, not just perform services. Add in its growing reputation, adaptable learning structure, and focus on current industry trends, and it makes a compelling case as a leading choice for aspiring estheticians across Canada.

Kalleigh Sharp, the owner and director behind the National Association of Estheticians, is often described as a passionate and driven leader whose impact goes far beyond the classroom. With a background from the University of Guelph and specialized training in services like lash extensions, microblading, and hair extensions, she combines technical expertise with strong marketing and business skills to shape the school’s curriculum and student experience. What makes her especially compelling is her entrepreneurial mindset—she not only teaches beauty techniques but also emphasizes independence, branding, and client-building, helping students turn their skills into real careers. Her journey into the industry, fueled by a desire for financial independence and empowering others to feel confident, adds a personal layer of authenticity to her work. Known for being hands-on in training, mentorship, and program development, Kalleigh Sharp plays a central role in creating a supportive, women-focused environment that encourages growth, creativity, and confidence in the next generation of estheticians.
