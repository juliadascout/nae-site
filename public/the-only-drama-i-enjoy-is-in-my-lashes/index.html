The only drama I enjoy is in my lashes

As society changes, so does supply and demand; societal change creates a demand for services that increase femininity. Today, the beauty industry capitalises on cosmetic appearance, medical or psychological concerns. 

Eyelash Anatomy

Long lashes are a beauty trend that draws attention to the eyes. Like they say, beauty is in the “eye” of the beholder. Long lashes create a wakeful look. Eyelash extensions are designed to extend the length, curl and fullness of natural eyelashes. Lash extensions are attached to the natural eyelash with a specially formulated lash extension adhesive.  Lash extensions are available in different lengths, widths and curls, as well as different colours. Classic lash extensions come in both standard (round) or flat (ellipse) styles. For volume lashes there are volume fans or premade volume clusters. Other options for fuller lashes include false strip lashes or temporary volume clusters. We provide a variety of lash extension training and lash services.
