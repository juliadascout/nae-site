Learn more about microblading.

Microblading is a semi-permanent cosmetic tattooing technique used to enhance the appearance of eyebrows. During a typical microblading session, a trained technician uses a fine blade to deposit pigment under the skin in hair-like strokes that mimic natural eyebrow hairs. This creates a more defined and fuller eyebrow shape, filling in gaps or sparse areas.

Microblading offers a natural and long-lasting solution for individuals with thin or overplucked eyebrows. The results typically last between 1 to 3 years, depending on individual factors such as skin type and aftercare.

Before undergoing microblading, it is important to consult with a qualified and experienced technician to ensure good results. Aftercare instructions, such as avoiding certain activities like swimming or excessive sweating, are usually provided to help maintain the longevity of the treatment.

Overall, microblading can be a great way to achieve natural-looking, fuller eyebrows without the need for daily makeup application.

Brow Lamination, Lift and Tint Artist Course

A full brow lift and tint treatment includes shaping the brows, waxing or sugaring, tweezing, tinting and brow lamination. Originating in the U.K. eyebrow lamination is sweeping the nation. It involves straightening the brow hairs using several solutions to create a full, thick, slicked, defined, fluffy brow shape. Custom looks can have a brow fade, raise a client's arch, extend their tails or give them a messy brow.
