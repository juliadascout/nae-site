Eyebrow Courses Hamilton Burlington

The Microblading Training course consists of theory & 20 hours of practical learning at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. There is no online option for practical training. Unfortunately, there is no practical location for this course in Hamilton or Burlington.

The Eyebrow Lamination, Lift and Tint course consists of an additional 6 hour workshop which is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. This course is also available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.

The Eyebrow Waxing course consists of theory & 5 hour workshop available at our St. Catharines/ Niagara location. This course is also available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.

The Eyebrow Threading course consists of a 5 hour workshop which is available at the St. Catharines/ Niagara location. This course is not available online for Hamilton, Burlington, Oakville, Toronto and Mississauga.

Eyebrow services isn't all fun and games. Microblading is extremely invasive and there is a high risk of infection. Clients also may be allergic to the microblading ink or eyebrow tint which will cause a horrible reaction. Know the risks first and work with a trained professional!
