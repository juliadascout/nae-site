Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026.

This policy explains what personal information National Association of Estheticians Inc. ("NAE", "we") collects through beautycourse.ca, our courses and our emails, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

1. Information we collect

What you give us. When you make an account, book a call, send an enquiry, enrol or pay, we collect your name, email address, phone number, the course and studio you are interested in, and anything you write to us. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see or store your full card number.

What we record as you use the site. Your account sign-ins, the courses you have bought, and basic technical information (such as your browser and approximate location from your IP address) that keeps the site secure and working.

Publicly available professional information. Many of the people we work with are beauty professionals or run their own studio. With your agreement to this policy, we may add publicly available professional details to your record, such as your business name, business website, or a public Instagram or LinkedIn profile. We use this only to understand which courses suit your work and to contact you about them. We do not collect anything that is private or behind a login, and you can ask us to remove it at any time.

2. How we use your information

  • To answer your questions, book calls, and enrol you in courses
  • To take payment, send receipts and course details, and issue your certificate of completion
  • To run your account and keep it secure
  • To improve our courses, studios and website
  • To send you news and offers by email, where you have agreed or where the law allows it (see section 4)

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run our business for us, and only what they need: Cloudflare (website hosting and data storage), Stripe (payments), Google Workspace (email and calendar), Google Analytics (site visit statistics) and Mailchimp (marketing email). Some of these providers store information outside Canada, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law. Each is bound to protect it. We may also disclose information where the law requires it.

4. Email from us

We send email about your enquiry, booking or purchase as part of serving you. We also send news and offers to people who have subscribed or who have a recent relationship with us, as CASL allows. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and we act on it within 10 business days. You can also email info@naeinc.ca to stop.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a student or prospective student, and afterwards for as long as we need it for records, taxes and certificates. When we no longer need it, we delete it or make it anonymous.

6. Keeping it safe

Your information is stored with access limited to the NAE staff and instructors who need it for their work. Accounts use strong passwords, and sign-ins to our staff tools need a second check.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can withdraw your consent to anything in this policy at any time. Email info@naeinc.ca and we will answer within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

8. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in and to make the site work. We also use Google Analytics, which sets cookies to tell us how many people visit and which pages they use; it does not tell us who you are. You can turn cookies off in your browser, or use Google's opt-out add-on for Analytics, but signing in will not work without cookies.

9. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and ask account holders to agree to the new version the next time they sign in.

10. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy go to our privacy contact at info@naeinc.ca.
